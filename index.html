About

A study platform built for real learning

Study Nova is a study and education application. It helps students turn raw material into structured courses, understand difficult pages, and check what they actually remember.

Instead of scattering notes, screenshots, quizzes, and diagrams across different tools, Study Nova keeps the whole learning flow in one place: build a course, learn from a photo, explore a Lab, quiz yourself, and finish with a mind map that makes the topic easy to recall.

The app is designed for students, self-learners, and anyone preparing for an exam who wants a calmer, more focused way to study.

Create

Turn notes, files, and photos into organised courses.

Understand

Get clear explanations and visual mind maps for each topic.

Practise

Use quizzes and Labs to test and reinforce your knowledge.
